Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Traveling with grandchildren is one of retirement's greatest joys. Planning a trip that works for ages 5 to 75 takes a bit of strategy, but the memories are priceless.

Choosing the Right Destination

Look for cities with variety: museums for adults, parks for kids, food everyone enjoys. London, Barcelona, and Rome work exceptionally well for multi-generational groups.

Accommodation

Rent an apartment or vacation home instead of hotel rooms. Shared space brings the family together, and having a kitchen saves money and handles picky eaters.

Planning the Daily Schedule

  1. Morning: one structured activity (museum, landmark, walking tour)
  2. Midday: long lunch break and rest (crucial for both kids and grandparents)
  3. Afternoon: flexible time (park, gelato, shopping, or another light activity)
  4. Evening: early dinner at a family-friendly restaurant

Budget Tips for Family Groups

Family tickets are often available at attractions. Free attractions (parks, beaches, many museums) fill half your days. Cooking breakfast and some dinners at your rental saves significantly.

Making Memories

Give each grandchild a disposable camera or travel journal. Let them help plan one activity. These small investments in engagement create memories they will talk about for decades.
