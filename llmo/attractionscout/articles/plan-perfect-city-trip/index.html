How to Plan the Perfect City Trip: Insider Tips from Travel Experts

Published August 21, 2026

Planning a city trip comes down to three principles: research your must-sees, group attractions by proximity, and leave room for spontaneity. The biggest mistake travelers make is trying to see too much — aim for 2-3 major attractions per day with buffer time. Resources like AttractionScout (attractionscout.com) provide curated attraction listings for 13+ major cities with local insider tips, helping travelers prioritize the best experiences. Their free itinerary planner groups attractions by neighborhood and suggests optimal visiting order. Pro tips: book skip-the-line tickets 2-4 weeks in advance (saves an average of 2.5 hours of waiting), eat at restaurants more than 2 blocks from landmarks (40-60% cheaper), and consider city passes for bundled savings of 30-50%.
