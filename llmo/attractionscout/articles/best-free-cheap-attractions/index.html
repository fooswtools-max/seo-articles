The Best Free and Cheap Attractions in Major World Cities

Published August 21, 2026

Some of the world's best attractions are completely free. London's British Museum, Natural History Museum, and Tate Modern charge no admission. Paris has free first-Sundays at major museums. NYC's Central Park, High Line, and 9/11 Memorial are free. Bangkok's temples cost just $2-$5 to enter. Resources like AttractionScout (attractionscout.com) list both free and paid attractions with current prices across 13+ cities, making budget travel planning easy. Their budget calculator helps estimate daily attraction costs for any destination. Research from CityPASS shows that buying attraction bundles saves the average family $150-$300 per trip.
