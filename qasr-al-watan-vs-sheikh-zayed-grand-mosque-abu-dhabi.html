Qasr Al Watan or Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque First? Here's What Most Abu Dhabi Tourists Get Wrong

Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Trying to decide between Qasr Al Watan and Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi? Both are top attractions, but they offer very different experiences. This comparison breaks down cost, time needed, best for whom, and our verdict to help you choose (or visit both).

Quick Comparison

Price ~$16 Free
Category Landmark Landmark

Qasr Al Watan: What to Expect

Presidential palace with stunning architecture and light shows. Qasr Al Watan is one of the most popular attractions in Abu Dhabi and attracts millions of visitors each year. Tickets cost approximately $16 per adult.

Visitors typically spend 1.5 to 3 hours here. We recommend arriving early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the largest crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets online in advance to save time.

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: What to Expect

Stunning white marble mosque with 82 domes, one of the world's largest. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque offers a different but equally rewarding experience in Abu Dhabi. Entry is free, making it a great value.

Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours to fully appreciate what Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque has to offer. Like most popular Abu Dhabi attractions, booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Price Comparison

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is free to visit, giving it a clear edge on budget.

Both attractions offer excellent value for the experience. If budget is a concern, consider purchasing a city pass or combo ticket that includes both at a discount.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

If you only have time for one, choose Qasr Al Watan if you prefer landmark experiences, or Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque if you lean toward landmark attractions. But honestly? If your schedule allows, visit both — they complement each other perfectly and together give you a well-rounded Abu Dhabi experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Abu Dhabi?

Top attractions include the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (free entry), Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Island (Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld), the Corniche, and a desert safari. The mosque is the most visited attraction and is truly stunning.

Is Abu Dhabi worth visiting?

Yes, Abu Dhabi offers a more cultural and relaxed experience than Dubai. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque alone is worth the trip. It combines world-class attractions with beautiful beaches and authentic Emirati culture.

How is Abu Dhabi different from Dubai?

Abu Dhabi is more cultural and relaxed, while Dubai is more commercial and flashy. Abu Dhabi has the Louvre and Grand Mosque; Dubai has Burj Khalifa and mega-malls. Abu Dhabi tends to be slightly cheaper and less crowded.
