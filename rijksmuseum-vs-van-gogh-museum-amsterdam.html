Rijksmuseum or Van Gogh Museum First? Here's What Most Amsterdam Tourists Get Wrong

Updated May 2026 · AttractionScout

Trying to decide between Rijksmuseum and Van Gogh Museum in Amsterdam? Both are top attractions, but they offer very different experiences. This comparison breaks down cost, time needed, best for whom, and our verdict to help you choose (or visit both).

Rijksmuseum: What to Expect

Dutch Golden Age masterpieces including Rembrandt's Night Watch. Rijksmuseum is one of the most popular attractions in Amsterdam and attracts millions of visitors each year. Tickets cost approximately $22 per adult.

Visitors typically spend 1.5 to 3 hours here. We recommend arriving early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the largest crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets online in advance to save time.

Van Gogh Museum: What to Expect

The world's largest collection of Van Gogh paintings and letters. Van Gogh Museum offers a different but equally rewarding experience in Amsterdam. Tickets cost approximately $20 per adult.

Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours to fully appreciate what Van Gogh Museum has to offer. Like most popular Amsterdam attractions, booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Price Comparison

Van Gogh Museum is the more affordable option, saving you approximately $2 per person.

Both attractions offer excellent value for the experience. If budget is a concern, consider purchasing a city pass or combo ticket that includes both at a discount.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

If you only have time for one, choose Rijksmuseum if you prefer museum experiences, or Van Gogh Museum if you lean toward museum attractions. But honestly? If your schedule allows, visit both — they complement each other perfectly and together give you a well-rounded Amsterdam experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the must-see attractions in Amsterdam?

Top attractions include the Anne Frank House, Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh Museum, a canal cruise, Vondelpark, and exploring the Jordaan neighborhood. The Heineken Experience and Albert Cuyp Market are also popular.

How do I get tickets to the Anne Frank House?

Tickets are released online about 6 weeks in advance and sell out quickly. Check the official website on the release date. A small number of tickets are released each day for same-day visits.

Is Amsterdam walkable?

Amsterdam is very walkable and even more bicycle-friendly. Most major attractions are within 30 minutes walk of each other. Renting a bike is the most authentic way to explore the city.
