Can I deduct student loan interest on my taxes?

Updated September 14, 2026 · StudLoans

For deduct student loan interest on my taxes, the first step is reviewing your federal loan servicer dashboard and exploring income-driven repayment options.

Understand Consolidation vs Refinancing

Federal loan consolidation combines your loans into one payment with a weighted average rate. Refinancing replaces your loans with a new private loan at a potentially lower rate but different terms.

Use the Avalanche Method to Save Money

Pay the minimum on all loans and direct extra money toward the highest interest rate loan first. This mathematically minimizes the total interest you pay over the life of your loans.

Look Into Employer Repayment Benefits

A growing number of employers now offer student loan repayment assistance as a benefit, with some providing up to $5,250 per year in tax-free contributions toward your loans.

Check State-Level Forgiveness Programs

Many states offer additional loan forgiveness or repayment assistance for healthcare workers, teachers, lawyers, and other professionals who work in underserved areas.

Know Your Loan Types

Federal and private student loans have completely different rules. Federal loans offer income-driven repayment plans, forgiveness programs, and deferment options that private loans do not provide.

Explore Income-Driven Repayment Plans

Under the SAVE plan, payments can be as low as 5% of discretionary income for undergraduate loans. These plans cap your payments based on what you can afford and offer forgiveness after 20-25 years.
