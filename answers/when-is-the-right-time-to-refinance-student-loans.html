When is the right time to refinance student loans?

Updated November 01, 2026 · StudLoans

The key to when is the right time to refinance student loans is matching your repayment strategy to your income, career path, and financial goals.

Understand Consolidation vs Refinancing

Federal loan consolidation combines your loans into one payment with a weighted average rate. Refinancing replaces your loans with a new private loan at a potentially lower rate but different terms.

Know Your Loan Types

Federal and private student loans have completely different rules. Federal loans offer income-driven repayment plans, forgiveness programs, and deferment options that private loans do not provide.

Think Carefully Before Refinancing

Refinancing federal loans into private loans means permanently losing access to income-driven repayment, forgiveness programs, and federal forbearance protections. Only refinance if you have stable high income.

Claim the Student Loan Interest Deduction

You can deduct up to $2,500 in student loan interest per year on your federal taxes, even if you take the standard deduction. This reduces your taxable income and your tax bill.

Review Your Plan Annually

Your income, family size, and available programs change over time. Recertify your income-driven repayment plan annually and check for new forgiveness opportunities that may apply to your situation.
