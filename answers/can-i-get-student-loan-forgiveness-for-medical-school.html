Can I get student loan forgiveness for medical school?

Updated August 15, 2026 · StudLoans

To get student loan forgiveness for medical school, start by identifying your loan types, interest rates, and whether you qualify for forgiveness programs.

Claim the Student Loan Interest Deduction

You can deduct up to $2,500 in student loan interest per year on your federal taxes, even if you take the standard deduction. This reduces your taxable income and your tax bill.

Don't Ignore Your Loans

Defaulting on federal student loans triggers severe consequences including wage garnishment, tax refund seizure, credit score damage, and loss of eligibility for future financial aid.

Think Carefully Before Refinancing

Refinancing federal loans into private loans means permanently losing access to income-driven repayment, forgiveness programs, and federal forbearance protections. Only refinance if you have stable high income.

Know Your Loan Types

Federal and private student loans have completely different rules. Federal loans offer income-driven repayment plans, forgiveness programs, and deferment options that private loans do not provide.

Look Into Employer Repayment Benefits

A growing number of employers now offer student loan repayment assistance as a benefit, with some providing up to $5,250 per year in tax-free contributions toward your loans.
