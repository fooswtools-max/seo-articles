Tsukiji Outer Market or Robot Restaurant First? Here's What Most Tokyo Tourists Get Wrong

Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Trying to decide between Tsukiji Outer Market and Robot Restaurant in Tokyo? Both are top attractions, but they offer very different experiences. This comparison breaks down cost, time needed, best for whom, and our verdict to help you choose (or visit both).

Tsukiji Outer Market: What to Expect

Legendary seafood market with the freshest sushi and street food. Tsukiji Outer Market is one of the most popular attractions in Tokyo and attracts millions of visitors each year. Entry is free, though some special exhibitions may have a fee.

Visitors typically spend 1.5 to 3 hours here. We recommend arriving early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the largest crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets online in advance to save time.

Robot Restaurant: What to Expect

Wild neon-lit show with dancing robots and performers in Shinjuku. Robot Restaurant offers a different but equally rewarding experience in Tokyo. Tickets cost approximately $60 per adult.

Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours to fully appreciate what Robot Restaurant has to offer. Like most popular Tokyo attractions, booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Price Comparison

Tsukiji Outer Market is free to visit, giving it a clear edge on budget.

Both attractions offer excellent value for the experience. If budget is a concern, consider purchasing a city pass or combo ticket that includes both at a discount.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

If you only have time for one, choose Tsukiji Outer Market if you prefer food experiences, or Robot Restaurant if you lean toward tour attractions. But honestly? If your schedule allows, visit both — they complement each other perfectly and together give you a well-rounded Tokyo experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best attractions in Tokyo?

Top attractions include Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, Shibuya Crossing, Meiji Shrine, Tsukiji Outer Market, Akihabara, Tokyo Skytree, and TeamLab Borderless. Day trips to Mount Fuji, Kamakura, and Nikko are highly recommended.

Is Tokyo expensive for tourists?

Tokyo is moderate. Street food and ramen are very affordable ($5-10 per meal). Many temples and shrines are free. Hotels range widely. The Japan Rail Pass saves money on day trips.

How many days do you need in Tokyo?

4-5 days is ideal. This covers major neighborhoods, temples, food markets, and allows for 1-2 day trips. Tokyo is large, so focus on 2-3 areas per day.
