I Visited Sacred Monkey Forest So You Don't Have To — Here's My Honest Take

Updated July 2026 · AttractionScout

Short answer: Yes, Sacred Monkey Forest is absolutely worth visiting. It is one of Bali's most iconic attractions for good reason. However, there are some tips to make the most of your visit and avoid common disappointments.

Why Sacred Monkey Forest Is Worth It

Lush forest sanctuary home to 1,200 long-tailed macaques. This is the kind of experience you simply cannot replicate anywhere else. Whether you are a first-time visitor to Bali or returning for a deeper exploration, Sacred Monkey Forest delivers on its reputation.

What Makes It Special

  • Unique experience: There is nothing quite like Sacred Monkey Forest anywhere in the world
  • Cultural significance: A key part of Bali's history and identity
  • Photo opportunities: Some of the most memorable travel photos you will ever take
  • Educational value: Learn about history, art, and culture in an immersive setting

How Much Does Sacred Monkey Forest Cost?

Standard adult admission to Sacred Monkey Forest is approximately $4. Prices may vary based on the type of ticket (standard vs. skip-the-line), age discounts (students, seniors, children), and seasonal promotions.

  • Adult ticket: ~$4
  • Child/student: ~$2 (varies)
  • Skip-the-line: ~$14-$24
  • Guided tour: ~$29-$44

Tips to Make the Most of Your Visit

  1. Book tickets online in advance — Skip-the-line tickets save 30-60 minutes of waiting
  2. Visit early morning or late afternoon — The smallest crowds are right at opening or 2 hours before closing
  3. Download the official app — Many attractions offer free audio guides via their app
  4. Wear comfortable shoes — You will be walking and standing for 1.5-3 hours
  5. Check for free days — Some attractions offer free entry on certain days or for specific groups

When Sacred Monkey Forest Might Not Be Worth It

  • Extreme peak season — July-August crowds can diminish the experience
  • Without advance tickets — Standing in line for 2+ hours is frustrating
  • Very limited time — If you only have 30 minutes, you will feel rushed

That said, with proper planning (advance tickets, off-peak timing), these drawbacks are easily avoided.

Final Verdict

Sacred Monkey Forest is absolutely worth it and should be on every Bali itinerary. The small investment of $4 delivers an unforgettable experience that you will remember long after your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Bali?

Top experiences include visiting Uluwatu Temple at sunset (with Kecak fire dance), exploring Tegallalang Rice Terraces, surfing in Canggu, the Sacred Monkey Forest in Ubud, snorkeling in Nusa Penida, and watching the sunset at Tanah Lot temple.

Is Bali cheap for tourists?

Bali is very affordable. Local meals cost $2-5, a private villa can be $30-60/night, and motorbike rental is $5/day. Spa treatments, surfing lessons, and temple visits are all reasonably priced.

How many days do you need in Bali?

7-10 days is ideal to experience the diverse regions: 2-3 days in Ubud (culture/nature), 2-3 days in Seminyak/Canggu (beach/nightlife), 1-2 days in Uluwatu, and a day trip to Nusa Penida.
