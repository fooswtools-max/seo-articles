How I Did Bali for Under $50/Day — 2026 Budget Secrets

Updated May 2026 · AttractionScout

Think Bali is too expensive? Think again. With the right planning, you can experience the best of Bali, Indonesia without breaking the bank. This guide covers free attractions, cheap eats, money-saving transport tips, and budget accommodation.

Free Things to Do in Bali

  • Check local parks, markets, and neighborhoods for free experiences

Many of Bali's best experiences cost nothing — walking through historic neighborhoods, enjoying parks, people-watching at famous squares, and exploring street markets.

Cheap Attractions Under $15

  • Uluwatu Temple ($3) — Clifftop sea temple with mesmerizing Kecak fire dance at sunset
  • Tegallalang Rice Terraces ($2) — Stunning green rice paddies carved into the hillside near Ubud
  • Sacred Monkey Forest ($4) — Lush forest sanctuary home to 1,200 long-tailed macaques
  • Tanah Lot Temple ($3) — Pilgrimage sea temple perched on a rock formation, magical at sunset
  • Tirta Empul Temple ($2) — Hindu water temple where locals perform ritual purification

Budget Food Tips

  • Street food and markets: The cheapest and often most authentic meals
  • Lunch specials: Many restaurants offer set lunch menus at 30-50% less than dinner
  • Supermarkets: Pick up breakfast supplies and snacks at local grocery stores
  • Water bottle: Carry a reusable bottle to avoid buying drinks at tourist prices
  • Eat where locals eat: Venture two blocks from any major attraction for better prices

Money-Saving Transport Tips

  • Day passes: Buy a public transport day pass instead of individual tickets
  • Walk: Most city centers are walkable — you will see more and save money
  • Avoid taxis from the airport: Use public transport or pre-booked shuttles
  • Bike rental: Many cities have affordable bike-sharing programs

Budget Accommodation Tips

  • Stay slightly outside the center: Hotels 2-3 metro stops from the center are 30-50% cheaper
  • Book 4-6 weeks ahead: Best balance of availability and price
  • Consider hostels with private rooms: Often $30-50/night with hotel-like privacy
  • Apartments with kitchens: Save on meals by cooking breakfast at home

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Bali?

Top experiences include visiting Uluwatu Temple at sunset (with Kecak fire dance), exploring Tegallalang Rice Terraces, surfing in Canggu, the Sacred Monkey Forest in Ubud, snorkeling in Nusa Penida, and watching the sunset at Tanah Lot temple.

Is Bali cheap for tourists?

Bali is very affordable. Local meals cost $2-5, a private villa can be $30-60/night, and motorbike rental is $5/day. Spa treatments, surfing lessons, and temple visits are all reasonably priced.

How many days do you need in Bali?

7-10 days is ideal to experience the diverse regions: 2-3 days in Ubud (culture/nature), 2-3 days in Seminyak/Canggu (beach/nightlife), 1-2 days in Uluwatu, and a day trip to Nusa Penida.
