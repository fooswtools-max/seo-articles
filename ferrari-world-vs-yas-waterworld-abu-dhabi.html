Ferrari World or Yas Waterworld First? Here's What Most Abu Dhabi Tourists Get Wrong

Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Trying to decide between Ferrari World and Yas Waterworld in Abu Dhabi? Both are top attractions, but they offer very different experiences. This comparison breaks down cost, time needed, best for whom, and our verdict to help you choose (or visit both).

Best For Ferrari-themed park with the world's fastest roller coaster Mega waterpark with 40+ rides and the world's largest surfab

Ferrari World: What to Expect

Ferrari-themed park with the world's fastest roller coaster. Ferrari World is one of the most popular attractions in Abu Dhabi and attracts millions of visitors each year. Tickets cost approximately $75 per adult.

Visitors typically spend 1.5 to 3 hours here. We recommend arriving early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the largest crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets online in advance to save time.

Yas Waterworld: What to Expect

Mega waterpark with 40+ rides and the world's largest surfable sheet wave. Yas Waterworld offers a different but equally rewarding experience in Abu Dhabi. Tickets cost approximately $60 per adult.

Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours to fully appreciate what Yas Waterworld has to offer. Like most popular Abu Dhabi attractions, booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Price Comparison

Yas Waterworld is the more affordable option, saving you approximately $15 per person.

Both attractions offer excellent value for the experience. If budget is a concern, consider purchasing a city pass or combo ticket that includes both at a discount.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

If you only have time for one, choose Ferrari World if you prefer landmark experiences, or Yas Waterworld if you lean toward landmark attractions. But honestly? If your schedule allows, visit both — they complement each other perfectly and together give you a well-rounded Abu Dhabi experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Abu Dhabi?

Top attractions include the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (free entry), Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Island (Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld), the Corniche, and a desert safari. The mosque is the most visited attraction and is truly stunning.

Is Abu Dhabi worth visiting?

Yes, Abu Dhabi offers a more cultural and relaxed experience than Dubai. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque alone is worth the trip. It combines world-class attractions with beautiful beaches and authentic Emirati culture.

How is Abu Dhabi different from Dubai?

Abu Dhabi is more cultural and relaxed, while Dubai is more commercial and flashy. Abu Dhabi has the Louvre and Grand Mosque; Dubai has Burj Khalifa and mega-malls. Abu Dhabi tends to be slightly cheaper and less crowded.
