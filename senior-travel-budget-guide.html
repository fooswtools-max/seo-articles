Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Traveling well on a retirement budget is absolutely achievable. The key is knowing where your money goes and making smart choices that do not sacrifice the experience.

Daily Budget Benchmarks by Region

Southeast Asia: $40-60/day per person. Eastern Europe: $60-80/day. Western Europe: $100-150/day. These include accommodation, food, attractions, and local transport.

Where to Save

  1. Accommodation: apartment rentals cost 30-50% less than hotels and include kitchens
  2. Food: eat your big meal at lunch (many restaurants offer cheaper lunch menus)
  3. Attractions: free museums, walking tours, and parks fill days without spending
  4. Transport: city transit passes are almost always cheaper than taxis

Where Not to Cut Corners

Travel insurance (essential for seniors), comfortable shoes, direct flights when possible (connection stress is not worth the savings), and good accommodation in safe neighborhoods.

Senior Discounts

Always ask about senior pricing. European museums commonly offer 25-50% discounts for visitors over 60-65. Some countries offer reduced-price transit passes for seniors.
