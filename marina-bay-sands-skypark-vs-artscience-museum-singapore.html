Marina Bay Sands SkyPark or ArtScience Museum First? Here's What Most Singapore Tourists Get Wrong

Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Trying to decide between Marina Bay Sands SkyPark and ArtScience Museum in Singapore? Both are top attractions, but they offer very different experiences. This comparison breaks down cost, time needed, best for whom, and our verdict to help you choose (or visit both).

Marina Bay Sands SkyPark: What to Expect

Iconic hotel rooftop with infinity pool views (observation deck open to public). Marina Bay Sands SkyPark is one of the most popular attractions in Singapore and attracts millions of visitors each year. Tickets cost approximately $26 per adult.

Visitors typically spend 1.5 to 3 hours here. We recommend arriving early in the morning or late in the afternoon to avoid the largest crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets online in advance to save time.

ArtScience Museum: What to Expect

Lotus-shaped museum with rotating exhibitions and permanent digital galleries. ArtScience Museum offers a different but equally rewarding experience in Singapore. Tickets cost approximately $19 per adult.

Plan for 1.5 to 3 hours to fully appreciate what ArtScience Museum has to offer. Like most popular Singapore attractions, booking in advance is strongly recommended.

Price Comparison

ArtScience Museum is the more affordable option, saving you approximately $7 per person.

Both attractions offer excellent value for the experience. If budget is a concern, consider purchasing a city pass or combo ticket that includes both at a discount.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

If you only have time for one, choose Marina Bay Sands SkyPark if you prefer landmark experiences, or ArtScience Museum if you lean toward museum attractions. But honestly? If your schedule allows, visit both — they complement each other perfectly and together give you a well-rounded Singapore experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the must-see attractions in Singapore?

Top attractions include Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove is free), Marina Bay Sands, Sentosa Island, Chinatown and Little India, the Singapore Botanic Gardens (free, UNESCO site), and a hawker center food tour.

Is Singapore expensive?

Singapore can be moderate. While hotels and taxis are pricey, hawker center meals cost $3-5, many gardens and temples are free, and public transport (MRT) is affordable. Attractions like Gardens by the Bay offer good value.

How many days do you need in Singapore?

3-4 days is enough to cover the main highlights. The city is compact and efficient, with excellent public transport connecting all major attractions.
