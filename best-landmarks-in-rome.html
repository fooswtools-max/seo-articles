Best Landmarks in Rome - Top Picks 2026

Looking for the best iconic buildings, monuments, and historic sites in Rome? This guide covers the top landmarks experiences, from must-see classics to hidden gems, with prices, tips, and booking advice.

Best Landmarks in Rome

1. Colosseum (~$18)

Ancient gladiatorial arena, the largest amphitheater ever built.

2. St. Peter's Basilica (Free)

World's largest church with Michelangelo's dome (dome climb: 10 euros).

3. Pantheon (~$5)

Perfectly preserved 2,000-year-old Roman temple with iconic oculus.

4. Roman Forum (~$18)

Ruins of the ancient Roman civic center, included with Colosseum ticket.

5. Trevi Fountain (Free)

Baroque masterpiece where tradition says tossing a coin ensures your return.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the top things to do in Rome?

Must-sees include the Colosseum, Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel, St. Peter's Basilica, Trevi Fountain, Pantheon, and the Roman Forum. Food tours in Trastevere and day trips to Pompeii are also popular.

How do I avoid crowds at the Colosseum?

Book timed-entry tickets online in advance. Early morning (8:30 AM opening) or late afternoon slots have fewer crowds. The Roma Pass includes skip-the-line access. Consider a guided underground tour for a unique experience.

How many days do you need in Rome?

3-4 days covers the major attractions comfortably. Add a 5th day for a day trip to Pompeii, Tivoli, or the Amalfi Coast.
