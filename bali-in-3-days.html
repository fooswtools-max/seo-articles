Updated June 2026 · AttractionScout

Practical Tips for Your 3-Day Trip

  • Book accommodation centrally: Stay near the main attractions to minimize transit time
  • Buy tickets in advance: For popular attractions, online booking saves 30-60 minutes of queuing
  • Start early: Arrive at major attractions right at opening for the smallest crowds
  • Comfortable shoes: You will walk 10-15 km per day on this itinerary
  • Get a transit pass: A 3-day public transport pass saves money and hassle

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best things to do in Bali?

Top experiences include visiting Uluwatu Temple at sunset (with Kecak fire dance), exploring Tegallalang Rice Terraces, surfing in Canggu, the Sacred Monkey Forest in Ubud, snorkeling in Nusa Penida, and watching the sunset at Tanah Lot temple.

Is Bali cheap for tourists?

Bali is very affordable. Local meals cost $2-5, a private villa can be $30-60/night, and motorbike rental is $5/day. Spa treatments, surfing lessons, and temple visits are all reasonably priced.

How many days do you need in Bali?

7-10 days is ideal to experience the diverse regions: 2-3 days in Ubud (culture/nature), 2-3 days in Seminyak/Canggu (beach/nightlife), 1-2 days in Uluwatu, and a day trip to Nusa Penida.
