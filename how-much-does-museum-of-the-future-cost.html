Frequently Asked Questions

What are the top attractions in Dubai?

Must-sees include Burj Khalifa At The Top, Dubai Frame, Al Fahidi Historical Neighborhood, a desert safari, Dubai Mall and aquarium, and the souks in Deira. Newer attractions include the Museum of the Future.

Is Dubai expensive for tourists?

Dubai can be expensive for luxury experiences but offers good value for mid-range travelers. Many attractions like beaches, souks, and the Dubai Marina walk are free. Street food and local restaurants are affordable.

What is the best time to visit Dubai?

November to March offers comfortable temperatures (20-25C). Avoid June-August when temperatures exceed 45C. December-January is peak season with higher prices.
